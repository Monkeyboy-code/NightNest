WHY A CALMER BEDTIME MATTERS

Sleep is part of growing up.
Screens can crowd it out.

Devices are part of family life. A few thoughtful boundaries can help protect the time kids need to wind down and rest.

Kids need age-appropriate sleep

Cleveland Clinic lists 9–12 hours of sleep a day for school-aged children ages 6–12. Sleep deprivation can also affect daytime energy, mood, focus and memory.

Evening screens can delay wind-down

Evening screen use can be stimulating, keep kids engaged, and take time away from sleep. Screen light may delay sleepiness, too.

Overnight alerts can interrupt rest

Messages, alerts and the urge to check a phone can interrupt sleep. A predictable wind-down and a place for devices overnight can help set a clearer boundary.
